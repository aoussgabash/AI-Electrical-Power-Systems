Lab 15 – Multi-Agent Energy Management

المخبر 15 – إدارة الطاقة متعددة الوكلاء

Objectives

  • Create three cooperative agents.
  • Exchange information.
  • Reach a consensus on battery power.

MATLAB Example

P = [10;20;30];
W = [0.5 0.25 0.25;
     0.25 0.5 0.25;
     0.25 0.25 0.5];

for k=1:20
    P = W*P;
end

disp(P)
