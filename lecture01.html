LECTURE 01

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Foundations, Concepts, and Applications in Electrical Power Systems

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lecture, students should be able to:

  • Explain the basic concept of Artificial Intelligence.
  • Distinguish AI from Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
  • Describe the main categories of AI systems.
  • Understand the concept of intelligent agents.
  • Identify important AI applications in electrical power systems.

1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science concerned with developing systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI systems can learn, reason, recognize patterns, make decisions, solve problems, and adapt to changing conditions.

In engineering, AI is especially useful for complex problems where conventional mathematical models are difficult, incomplete, or computationally expensive.

2. Brief History of AI

1950s

Early ideas about machine intelligence and symbolic reasoning.

1980s

Expert systems became an important AI application.

1990s-2000s

Machine learning gained increasing importance.

2010s-Present

Deep learning, big data, and high-performance computing accelerated AI development.

3. AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning

Artificial Intelligence

The broad field concerned with creating intelligent computer systems.

Machine Learning

A branch of AI in which systems learn patterns and relationships directly from data.

Deep Learning

A branch of Machine Learning based mainly on multi-layer neural networks.

Artificial Intelligence → Machine Learning → Deep Learning

4. Main Types of AI Methods

Search Algorithms

Finding solutions within large spaces of possible alternatives.

Fuzzy Logic

Reasoning with uncertainty and linguistic information.

Machine Learning

Learning models and patterns from data.

Artificial Neural Networks

Computational models inspired by biological neural systems.

Genetic Algorithms

Optimization methods inspired by biological evolution.

Deep Learning

Advanced neural-network models for complex data and large-scale learning.

5. Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent observes its environment through sensors, processes information, and takes actions to achieve a specific goal.

Environment → Sensors → Intelligent Agent → Actions

In electrical power systems, an intelligent controller can monitor voltage, frequency, power flow, renewable generation, and battery state and then select an appropriate control action.

6. Why AI in Electrical Power Systems?

Modern power systems are becoming increasingly complex because of renewable energy sources, distributed generation, battery storage, electric vehicles, smart meters, and active consumers.

AI can support faster and more adaptive operation when traditional analytical methods alone are not sufficient.

7. AI Applications in Electrical Power Systems

Load Forecasting

Predicting future electricity demand.

Renewable Forecasting

Prediction of PV and wind generation.

Fault Detection

Identification and classification of network faults.

Voltage Control

Intelligent control of voltage profiles.

Optimal Power Flow

AI-supported optimization of network operating points.

Battery Energy Storage

Intelligent charging, discharging, scheduling, and energy management.

8. Summary

  • AI enables computers to perform intelligent tasks.
  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning are important branches of AI.
  • AI includes fuzzy logic, neural networks, genetic algorithms, and search methods.
  • Electrical power systems offer many important applications for AI.
  • AI can improve prediction, control, monitoring, and optimization.
