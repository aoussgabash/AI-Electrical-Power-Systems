Lab 16 – Building a Simple Digital Twin

المخبر 16 – بناء توأم رقمي مبسط

Objectives

  • Simulate streaming measurements.
  • Update the digital twin every second.
  • Detect abnormal behavior.

MATLAB

t=0:0.1:100;
P=100+10*sin(0.2*t)+randn(size(t));
threshold=115;
alarm=P>threshold;
plot(t,P); hold on;
yline(threshold,'r--');
